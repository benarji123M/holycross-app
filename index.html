Paid sick leave

—
Statutory sick pay in Ireland is 70% of your normal daily pay, up to €110 a day, after 13 weeks with the same employer. The number of paid days is set by law and it has changed over the years. I set 5 days. Check the current number on gov.ie or citizensinformation.ie, and change it here if it is different. Counted per calendar year.
